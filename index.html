01 — About

From the shop floor
to the dashboard.

I started in MIS reporting at Peekay Rolling Mills, a manufacturing environment where sales, distribution, and financial data lived in disconnected ERP exports and scattered Excel sheets, reconciled by hand every reporting cycle. My job became closing that gap — building Power BI models and DAX measures that turned that data into dashboards leadership could explore themselves, instead of waiting on a fresh spreadsheet every time.

That experience taught me the part of BI that doesn't show up in a tutorial: messy real-world data, stakeholders who want one number but need five, and the difference between a dashboard that looks good and one that gets opened every Monday morning.

I'm currently based in Fujairah, UAE, pursuing an MBA in Data Science alongside hands-on project work, and looking for my next BI / Data Analyst role in the region.

Sales & Distribution Performance Dashboard

Power BI · DAX · Excel · ERP Data

Multi-page sales tracking system for Peekay Rolling Mills, consolidating ERP exports into a live "Sales Meter" view that management could explore by zone, dealer, and sub-dealer — replacing a manual Excel-based consolidation process that ran on every reporting cycle.

  • Built gauge-based target-completion tracking for each zone (Kozhikode, Malappuram, Kannur), showing sales-till-date against monthly targets and required run rate in real time

Retail Sales & Consumer Analytics Dashboard

Power BI · DAX · Power Query · Data Modeling

⚠ Built on simulated demo data · Futuralabs Technologies internship

Three-page Power BI report tracking end-to-end retail performance — from top-line revenue and product category mix through to channel goal attainment and consumer segmentation — built to give a management team a single source of truth across a multi-product, multi-channel retail operation.

  • Tracked revenue earned, quantities sold, and unique customer count (current year vs prior year) using monthly sparklines to surface growth and decline patterns across a 4-year period
  • Broke down performance across three product verticals — Fashion & Lifestyle, Electronics & Gadgets, and Home & Leisure — with sub-category drill-through identifying top revenue contributors such as Audio/Video, Books, and Personal Appliances
  • Measured channel goal attainment across four sales channels — e-Shop, Flagship Store, MBR, and Tele-shop — with actual-vs-target variance and a product sub-category contribution matrix per channel
  • Segmented customers by gender and four age groups (Established Adults, Young Professionals, Seasoned Professionals, Emerging Adults), tracked year-over-year acquisition rates, monthly purchase frequency patterns, and peak sales months

Logistics & supply chain

Supply Chain KPI Dashboard

Tableau · Supply Chain Analytics · Logistics Data

⚠ Built on simulated demo data · Futuralabs Technologies internship

End-to-end supply chain operations dashboard in Tableau tracking product economics, inventory health, supplier performance, and carrier-level cost and revenue across a multi-modal logistics network — filterable by transport mode (Air, Rail, Road, Sea) and region (Bangalore, Chennai, Delhi, Kolkata, Mumbai).

  • Compared product cost, manufacture cost, demand share, item count, and gross profit across three product lines (Cosmetics, Haircare, Skincare) — surfacing that Skincare led on revenue and profit despite the highest cost base, while Cosmetics captured the largest demand share at 39%
  • Monitored SKU-level stock positions flagging low-availability items in a ranked table, and benchmarked five suppliers across two operational KPIs to identify performance outliers in the supply base
  • Broke down revenue generated and cost incurred by three carriers (Carrier A, B, C) across Air, Rail, Road, and Sea modes — enabling cost-vs-revenue analysis by route type to surface over- and under-performing logistics legs
  • Transport mode and city filters allow any team — whether regional ops or central planning — to drill into their specific geography-route combination without a separate report
